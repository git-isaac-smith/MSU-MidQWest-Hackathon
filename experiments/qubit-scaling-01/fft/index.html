What transferred to larger grids

The site CNN weights and thresholds were frozen at d5. The direct FFT site parity error rose from 14.92% to 45.12%. Terminal spatial matching cut the observed error markedly. Zero failures on 1,024 shots have a 95% Wilson upper bound of 0.374%. The logical CNN and exact posterior paths in this experiment use the d5 configuration.

The frozen d7 list recipe routed 209, 3, 0 and 0 shots to its 49-match search at d5, d9, d17 and d33. Each large-distance call still evaluated the gap probe.

Timing protocol

Intel Core i7-13700HX, one process, BLAS and OpenMP thread limits of one. We interleaved all decoders over the same five distinct 64-record chunks after one warm-up chunk. Time includes inference from unpacked detector bits and excludes setup, Stim generation, unpacking and scoring. Accuracy uses the full held-out archive. Every timed output matched the corresponding archived prediction. Small batches, the trigger frequency and host load affect runtime. These results are for the controlled prototype circuits.
